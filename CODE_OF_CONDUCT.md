# Community conduct

People must be able to ask basic questions, challenge a decision, and report a mistake without being targeted personally. In issues, reviews, and discussions, address the work, explain your reasoning, and respect requests to stop. Harassment, threats, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

For a disruptive public post, use GitHub’s reporting menu on the post to reach the repository administrators when that option is available. For abuse, threats, or private information, you can [report directly to GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Avoid reposting sensitive material in a public issue.

Maintainers may hide or remove content, lock a conversation, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Anyone involved in a report should share its details only with people needed to address it.
